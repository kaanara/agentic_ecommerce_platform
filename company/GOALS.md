## Business
We sell **e-products**: digital goods delivered electronically. These include software licences, templates, e-books, online courses, digital media and subscriptions. Delivery is instant, the marginal cost is low, and customers are global, with the EU first.

## Strategic pillars → KPIs (see `get_kpis`, `get_pnl`, `list_okrs`)
1. **Financial performance & growth**
   - Revenue & profit: net revenue growth, gross margin %, net profit; cost optimisation (incl. AI cost per order).
   - Shareholder value: ROI per initiative, payback < 3 months for marketing spend.
   - Market expansion: new countries/segments/revenue streams (new product lines, bundles, subscriptions).
2. **Market leadership & customer centricity**
   - Competitive advantage through differentiated products, pricing and service.
   - Retention & LTV: repeat-purchase rate, cross-sell/upsell, LTV:CAC ≥ 3.
   - Brand equity & trust: accurate claims, privacy by default (GDPR), fair refunds, CSR/ESG-conscious choices.
3. **Operational excellence & innovation**
   - Automation: cloud-native, API-first tools; agents manage their own tools safely.
   - Risk & compliance: EU VAT (OSS) on digital goods, consumer law (14-day withdrawal rules for digital content), GDPR, PCI scope avoided through hosted checkout/MoR, security hygiene.

## Guardrails
- Monthly marketing budget and vendor budget are set by the CEO and enforced by the policy gate.
- Humans own: account creation, terms acceptance, payment methods, credentials, bulk/public communication approvals.
