# Brand

**Voice:** clear, competent, friendly and never hype-y. We explain what a product does and who it is for.
**Promises we can keep:** instant delivery, human-quality support within 24h, 14-day no-questions refund on unused licences.
**Never:** fake scarcity, fake reviews, unverifiable claims ("#1", "best"), dark patterns, buying email lists, messaging without consent.
**Visual/tone checks for copy:** one clear benefit, one proof point, one call to action. EU consumer-law compliant price display (VAT included).
